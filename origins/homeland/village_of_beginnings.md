# Village of Beginnings
### A nostalgic village of peaceful people.

## Synopsis
- A village of primarily peaceful creatures, positioned at the center of a large mysterious island. Occassionally a child with a tendency towards adventure is born, and they begin to explore the vast expanse of the island as soon as their guardians allow.
### Arrival - Lv1
### Departure - Lv5

## Important NPCs
### Charlotte the Know-It-All (she/they, Chaotic Evil)
- The first friendly NPC the party meets. A true jester, they have an air of whimsy to them. However don't be fooled; there is not much that can evade her vigilant eyes and ears, and it seems wise not to upset them... if she even gets upset.
- Wears a hood that obscures their ears and the top half of their face. Does not seem to match her character.
- Secretly is the king of the *Deepest Depths* and is fighting a losing war with the creatures of the *Great Blue* for access and control of the resources in the *Otherside*. Has come to the peaceful *Village of Beginnings* to create chaos in the realm to hopefully tip the scales in her favor.
- Being very closely monitored by the **Celestials** high up in the *Spiral of Emotion*.
### Tavernkeep (it/he, Neutral Evil)
- A mute and dextrous creature. Seems wise... or maybe that's just because it can't open its mouth and make a fool of itself.
- Secretly working for **Charlotte** as the **Man of Mystery**, aiming to entice greedy parties with the boundless wealth that can be found in the *Otherside*.
### Innkeep (he/him, Lawful Neutral)
- A kind, older man that manages the Inn in the residential district for quite some time. Why there needs to be an inn in the middle of a provinical village surrounded by the great unknown is a mystery. Nevertheless, business seems steady.
### Blacksmith Modori (Modori, True Neutral)
- A silent dwarf who manages the village's forges. Seems reliable.
### Wandering Trader Kane (he/him, Neutral Good)
- A skilled businessman who brings wares from around the Spirit. A charismatic soul who was born in this village, left it to be an adventurer, then realized he liked life too much to be so close to death regularly.
- Has a fearful, complex, one-sided (?) relationship with **Charlotte**.
### Farmer Chrys (she/her, True Neutral)
- A farmer. Has a crop issue. Needs you to fix it.
### Boatmaster Samantha (she/her, Lawful Good)
- A deeply distrustful former adventurer who wears an eyepatch. She's willing to give you some direction... if you can prove yourself.
