# Village of Beginnings
### A nostalgic village of peaceful people.

## Synopsis
- A land of primarily peaceful creatures, positioned at the center of a large mysterious island. Occassionally a child with a tendency towards adventure is born, and they begin to explore the vast expanse of the island as soon as their guardians allow.
